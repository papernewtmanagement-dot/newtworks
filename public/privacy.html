Applicant Privacy Notice

Effective July 30, 2026

This notice explains how Peter Story State Farm ("the agency," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide when you apply for a job with our agency — whether directly on our careers site or through Indeed's Apply integration.

Information we collect

When you apply, we receive the information you submit as part of your application. This may include:

  • Your name and contact information (email, phone, mailing address)
  • Your resume and any attached documents
  • Your work history, education, and qualifications
  • Your answers to any screener questions we include with the job posting
  • Optional information you choose to share, such as a cover letter

When you apply through Indeed, this information is delivered to us by Indeed on your behalf using their Indeed Apply integration.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit for these purposes only:

  • To evaluate your fit for the role you applied to and other open roles at the agency
  • To contact you about your application, schedule interviews, and communicate hiring decisions
  • To keep records required by applicable employment law

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Your application is visible only to the agency owner and authorized hiring team members at Peter Story State Farm.

We use the following service providers to store and process application data on our behalf. They act as data processors under our instructions and do not use your information for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — application database and file storage
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Indeed — for applications submitted through Indeed Apply, until delivered to us

We may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or to protect the legal rights of the agency.

How long we keep your information

We retain application records for as long as your application remains active, plus a reasonable period thereafter to comply with employment recordkeeping laws — typically no longer than three years from the date you last applied, unless a longer retention period is legally required.

Your choices and rights

You may request that we delete your application information at any time by emailing us at the address below. If you applied through Indeed, you may also manage your Indeed account and data directly with Indeed at indeed.com.

Deleting your application means we can no longer consider you for open roles at the agency. If you wish to reapply later, you will need to submit a new application.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your application information from unauthorized access, including access controls on our database, encrypted connections for all data transfer, and role-based permissions for anyone at the agency who reviews applications.

Children

Our hiring system is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect information from applicants under that age.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The "Effective" date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised.
